# Cascade for Chrome — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 18 November 2026_

Cascade for Chrome ("the extension") lets runs on your Cascade server use your Chrome browser, with your permission each time. This policy says what the extension handles, where it goes, and what it never does.

## What the extension sends, and when

Only while a run you have approved is open, and only from that run's own window, the extension sends to **your Cascade server** (the one you paired with, such as cascadeai.in or a server you host):

- the addresses of pages in the run's window;
- the text and structure of those pages;
- what the run types or clicks in that window.

This is sent so the run can carry out the task you gave it. Nothing is sent from your other tabs or windows, and nothing at all when no run is open. No screenshots or recordings of your screen are taken or sent.

## What stays on your computer

- **The device key.** Pairing makes a key on your computer; only its public half is sent to your server. The private key stays in the extension's storage.
- **The server address and this browser's name**, which you choose when pairing.
- The state of the current run, removed when it ends.

## What the extension never does

- It doesn't read your cookies, saved passwords, browser storage or downloads, or send them anywhere.
- It doesn't act in any tab you have open; a run gets a new window of its own.
- It doesn't sell or share data with anyone, and doesn't use it for advertising, profiling or credit decisions.
- It contains no analytics or tracking.

## Your Cascade server

What happens to data once it reaches your Cascade server is covered by that server's own terms: for cascadeai.in, its privacy policy; for a server you host, your own arrangements. The runs themselves, and the AI models they use, are configured there.

## Your choices

- Say no when a run asks, or allow only the sites you want.
- Take over a run, or stop it at any time from the toolbar button or by closing its window.
- "Forget this browser" in the extension deletes its key; remove the device from your account on your server too. Uninstalling the extension removes everything it stored.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published here, with its date above.
